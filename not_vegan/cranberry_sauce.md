
## NAME
Orange Cranberry Sauce with Brown Sugar, Cinnamon & Vanilla

## Servings
24–30 servings (approximately 7–8 cups)

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
20–25 minutes

## Ingredients
* 3 lb (48 oz) fresh cranberries
* 2 cups granulated sugar
* 1 cup light brown sugar, packed
* 1½ cups fresh orange juice
* ½ cup water
* Zest from 3 oranges
* 3 cinnamon sticks
* 2 tsp vanilla extract
* ½ tsp kosher salt
* 2–3 tbsp Grand Marnier or bourbon (optional)

## Directions
1. In a large heavy-bottomed pot, combine the cranberries, granulated sugar, brown sugar, orange juice, water, orange zest, cinnamon sticks, and salt.
2. Bring to a boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to medium-low. Simmer uncovered for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the cranberries burst and the sauce begins to thicken.
3. Lightly mash about one-third of the cranberries with a wooden spoon, leaving the rest whole for a rustic texture. Simmer another 3–5 minutes if needed.
4. Remove from heat and discard the cinnamon sticks. Stir in the vanilla extract and optional Grand Marnier or bourbon.
5. Taste while warm and adjust sweetness if necessary, adding sugar 2 tablespoons at a time.
6. Allow to cool, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight, before serving.
7. Serve chilled or at cool room temperature, optionally garnished with fresh orange zest.

Storage: Refrigerate in airtight containers for 7–10 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
